Profile

Data analyst and data scientist turning messy raw data into clear, decision-ready dashboards, models and stories.

I clean up messy tables, build models people can trust, and design dashboards that answer the question before it gets asked. Most of my work sits in the unglamorous part of the job: reconciling exports that disagree, fixing the definitions nobody wrote down, and rebuilding the metrics layer so the numbers mean the same thing on Monday as they did on Friday.

The rest is where it gets fun — modelling demand, sizing experiments, and shipping dashboards that get used daily instead of admired once. I care about clean data contracts, reproducible queries, and charts a stakeholder can read in five seconds without me in the room.
